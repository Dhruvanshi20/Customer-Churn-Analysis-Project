## 📌 Problem Statement
A telecom company is experiencing customer churn, which directly impacts revenue and business growth.  
The objective of this project is to analyze customer behavior, identify the key factors driving churn, and provide business recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- SQL
- Tableau

---

## 📊 Dataset Overview
The dataset contains customer-related information such as:
- Customer tenure
- Contract type
- Monthly charges
- Churn status
- Payment and subscription details

---

## 📈 Analysis Performed
The project focuses on:
- Overall churn rate analysis
- Churn analysis by contract type
- Customer segmentation based on tenure
- Churn analysis by monthly charges
- Identification of high-risk customers

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Customers with **month-to-month contracts** show the highest churn rate.
- **New customers** are more likely to churn compared to long-term customers.
- Customers with **higher monthly charges** tend to leave more frequently.
- High churn is mainly driven by:
  - Lack of long-term commitment
  - Early customer dissatisfaction
  - Pricing sensitivity

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Encourage customers to move toward long-term contracts through offers and discounts.
- Improve onboarding and support for new customers.
- Re-evaluate pricing strategies for high-paying customers.
- Create targeted retention campaigns for high-risk customer groups.

---

## 📊 Dashboard
The Tableau dashboard provides:
- KPI overview
- Churn analysis by contract type
- Churn analysis by tenure
- Churn analysis by monthly charges
- Average monthly charges by churn status

---

🎯 Conclusion

This project highlights how customer churn can be analyzed using SQL and Tableau to uncover meaningful business insights.
The analysis helps identify high-risk customer segments and provides actionable recommendations to improve customer retention and reduce revenue loss.
